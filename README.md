## TimeSpell Oracle

Calculator for my experimental synchronometer "TimeSpell Oracle".<br>
Entirely based on the [DreamSpell](https://github.com/damienjoldersma/DreamSpell) web app by [Damien Joldersma](https://github.com/damienjoldersma).<br>

### TimeSpell Oracle correlation:<br>
* Anchor Dates: ***August 16-17, 1987*** (Harmonic Convergence).<br>
* 16 August 1987 is 13 Sun, Day Out of Time, year 13 Warrior (End of Cycle).
* 17 August 1987 is 1 Dragon, day 1 of Magnetic Moon, year 1 Dragon (Start of Cycle).<br>
* New Year: August 17. Dates before August 17 belong to the preceding TimeSpell year.<br>
* Day tone: Dreamspell day tone minus 3, wrapped into 1 through 13.<br>
* Day seal: Dreamspell day seal plus 5, wrapped into 1 through 20.<br>
* Year tone and seal are the TimeSpell tone and seal of August 17 starting that year.<br>
* The oracle, wavespell, and kin are calculated from the TimeSpell day tone and seal.<br>
* The original Dreamspell leap-day/day-count convention is retained, so the offsets<br>
  stay constant relative to the original program on every date.<br>

* **José Arguelles** *("Valum Votan")* was born on Kin 216, Yellow Galactic Warrior,<br>
  whose Guide is Kin 60, Yellow Galactic Sun (Pacal Votan);<br>
* **Stephanie South** *("Red Queen")* was born on Kin 130, White Cosmic Dog.
* The TimeSpell Year 2012-2013 was White Cosmic World-Bridger, Kin 26, one of the 13<br>
  Clear Signs of Pacal Votan, and its Guide is White Cosmic Dog, Kin 130, the birth Kin<br>
  of Stephanie South.
